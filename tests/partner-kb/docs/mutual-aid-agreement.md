# Mutual aid agreement

The museum and the Institute undertake to give each other space, freezer capacity and trained hands
in an emergency, without a purchase order. Anything that requires a purchase order will not happen
on the night.

The agreement is reviewed annually and tested every second year, in an exercise where neither party
is warned of the scenario. An untested mutual aid agreement is a statement of goodwill.

Each party keeps the other's plan, contact list and building access arrangements on paper.
