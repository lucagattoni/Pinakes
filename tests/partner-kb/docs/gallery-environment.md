# Gallery environment

Galleries are held at 40–60% relative humidity, with change limited to 5% in twenty-four hours. The
rate of change matters more than the value: materials tolerate a steady wrong number better than a
correct one that moves.

Light on works on paper is capped at 50 lux with ultraviolet filtered out, and exposure is counted
cumulatively across exhibitions. Light damage does not reverse, so the budget is annual and spent
once.
