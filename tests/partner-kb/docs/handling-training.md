# Handling training

Nobody moves an object unsupervised until they have been trained and observed, volunteers and
trustees included. Most damage is done by people who were being helpful.

Training covers what not to touch as much as how to lift. The instinct to steady a wobbling object
with a hand is the instinct that breaks it.

Records of who has been trained are kept with the rota, so an untrained pair of hands on a Sunday is
visible before the object is.
