# Collections review

Review asks what the museum can care for, not what it would like to own. A collection larger than
the store is a collection being damaged in the corridor.

Disposal is by transfer to another accredited institution first, and is never a route to income for
day-to-day costs. Selling to pay the electricity bill is how a collection is spent.

Every review decision is minuted with its reasoning, because the objection arrives a decade later
and the reasoning is what answers it.
