# Accession workflow

An item arrives at the loading room, is logged against its donor, and waits in quarantine for ten
working days. Quarantine exists to catch pests and damp before either can reach the stacks.

After quarantine the item goes to the accessions desk for a catalogue number, then to conservation
for assessment. Only then is it shelved. Skipping the conservation step is the most common cause of
an item being reshelved twice.
